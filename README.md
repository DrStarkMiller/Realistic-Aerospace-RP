## New Terms created for this RP (Non-Official/Not used in NASA):

- Close Flyby Trajectory (CFT)
 • A flyby with a periapsis from 1km to 100m.

- Close Flyby Orbit (CFO)
 • A orbit with a periapsis from 1km to 100m and a apoapsis at least over 100km or more. (Depends on the planet)
 
- Eccentric [Planet Name] Orbit (EC_O)
 • A orbit with a eccentricity between 0.75 to 0.9 eccentricity, also used for planet orbits.
 
- Very High [Planet Name] Orbit (VH_O)
 • A orbit above 75% of the SOI diameter. (Eg: SOI of certain body is 25,000m, VHO of that object would be 6,250.)

- SOI Edge Orbit (SEO)
 • A orbit within only 50m of distance from the craft to the SOI edge.
  
- Near-Impact Flyby (NIF)
 • A term for a flyby/gravity assist within 20m off the surface.
